## Project Summary

This project is an AI phone receptionist for home-service businesses, starting with pest control and later expanding into HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and similar local service categories.

The assistant answers inbound calls 24/7, qualifies the caller, captures booking details, logs the request into Google Sheets, and sends post-call SMS notifications. The current stack is Twilio, Deepgram, Claude Haiku, Cartesia, Pipecat, and Fly.io. The product is in soft launch with Clear Slope Pest Control and is being packaged for the first external paying clients.

The core sales promise:

> We help home-service businesses stop losing revenue from missed calls, voicemail, slow response times, and after-hours inquiries.

## Opportunity Post

We are looking for one or two serious independent sales partners to help bring a 24/7 AI phone receptionist to home-service businesses.

The product is built for pest control, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and similar local service businesses that lose money whenever calls go to voicemail, ring unanswered, or arrive after hours. Our AI receptionist answers the phone, qualifies the lead, captures booking details, logs the request, and sends SMS follow-up so the owner can recover jobs they would otherwise miss.

This is not a generic chatbot. It is a live phone receptionist system built on Twilio, Deepgram, Claude, Cartesia, and Fly.io, with real-time voice conversation, client-specific business hours, intake logging, emergency tagging, and post-call notifications.

### Why This Is a Strong Sales Opportunity

- Clear pain: local service businesses lose revenue when inbound calls are missed.
- Simple buyer: usually the owner or operator, not a committee.
- Easy wedge: start with after-hours or missed-call handling instead of replacing their current staff.
- Low-risk pilot offer: "Forward only after-hours calls for 30 days and measure captured bookings."
- Strong pricing room: plans range from $199/mo to $999/mo plus setup.
- High gross margin: estimated 80%+ gross margin at normal usage.
- Expansion path: dashboard, call transcripts, smart scheduling, route optimization, outbound reminders, and CRM/FSM integrations.

### Ideal Customers

The first focus is local home-service companies with meaningful inbound call volume:

- Pest control
- Wildlife exclusion
- HVAC
- Plumbing
- Roofing
- Electrical
- Lawn care
- Locksmiths
- Restoration and emergency services

The best prospects already spend money on ads, have missed-call or after-hours leakage, and know each new booked job is worth real money.

### Sales Motion

The recommended entry offer is a 30-day after-hours pilot:

> "Forward only your after-hours calls to the AI receptionist for 30 days. Those calls are currently going to voicemail anyway. We will send a weekly recap showing calls handled, leads captured, and estimated recovered revenue."

This keeps the owner's risk low and gives the salesperson a concrete ROI story.

### Compensation

Commission structure:

- 50% of the setup fee
- 50% of all subscription revenue for the first 12 months of the account
- 5% recurring residual on subscription revenue from month 13 onward, for the life of the account, while the rep remains in good standing

All commission is paid monthly as revenue is collected. No prepayment of annual amounts. If the client cancels, commission stops on the cancelled portion, the rep keeps everything earned to date.

Current pricing:

- Starter: $199/mo
- Pro: $499/mo
- Premium: $999/mo
- Setup: $299-$499 one time

Example earnings per closed client:

- Starter ($299 setup, $199/mo): ~$149 setup + ~$99.50/mo year 1 + ~$9.95/mo from month 13. About $1,343 in year 1, ~$119/yr ongoing.
- Pro ($399 setup, $499/mo): ~$199 setup + ~$249.50/mo year 1 + ~$24.95/mo from month 13. About $3,193 in year 1, ~$299/yr ongoing.
- Premium ($499 setup, $999/mo): ~$249 setup + ~$499.50/mo year 1 + ~$49.95/mo from month 13. About $6,243 in year 1, ~$599/yr ongoing.

For the right partner, we can discuss territory focus, vertical focus, and higher residuals for self-sourced accounts that reach meaningful volume.

## Important Positioning Notes

Do not pitch this as replacing a receptionist on day one. That creates fear and objections.

Pitch it as capturing missed revenue:

- After-hours calls
- Overflow calls
- Missed calls
- Busy season spikes
- Weekend inquiries

The safest first promise is:

> We answer calls you are already missing and show you what they were worth.
