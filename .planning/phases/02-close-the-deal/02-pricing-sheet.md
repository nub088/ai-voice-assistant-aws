## What's Included (All Tiers)

- AI answers every call 24/7, no hold music, no voicemail
- Attempts to book an appointment on every call
- Sends SMS confirmation to the caller after booking
- Logs every call with transcript to a Google Sheet you own
- Custom greeting and service menu (your business name, your services)
- No hardware, no software to install, works with your existing phone number via call forwarding

## Setup Fee

**$299–$499** one-time charge.

Covers: prompt customization for your business, Twilio number provisioning, Google Sheets integration, 30-minute onboarding call.

This is a one-time cost. Monthly subscription has no hidden fees.

## Which Tier Is Right for You?

For All Green Pest Control, we recommend **Starter ($199/mo)**.

All Green's after-hours call volume (33–46 calls/month) translates to roughly 100–180 minutes of AI call time per month, well within the 500-minute Starter ceiling. No overage expected.

Payback math: A single recovered pest control job (**$150–$300** average ticket) at a 30% close rate pays for **3+ months** of Starter.
