# AI Phone Receptionist, ROI Sheet
## How much revenue is your phone leaking?

A one-page worksheet to estimate captured-call ROI for home-service businesses. Use this on a sales call, fill in the blanks with the prospect, then compare to plan cost.

## Worked Example: Small Pest Control Operator

A 3–5 truck pest control business, owner answers calls personally, no after-hours answering service.

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Inbound calls per week | 25 |
| Estimated missed rate (after-hours + overflow + busy) | 30% (industry: 27–38%) |
| Missed calls per month | ~32 |
| Average ticket (one-time service) | $275 |
| Conversion on answered call | 45% |
| **Revenue leaked per month** | **~$3,960** |
| Revenue leaked per year | ~$47,500 |

### What the AI Receptionist Captures

Assume the AI captures 70% of missed calls into qualified leads (conservative, calls are answered live, not voicemail), and converts at the same 45% rate as a human-answered call.

| Calculation | Value |
|---|---|
| Missed calls/mo captured by AI | ~22 |
| Bookings recovered/mo | ~10 |
| **Recovered revenue/mo** | **~$2,750** |
| Recovered revenue/yr | ~$33,000 |

### ROI vs Cost

| | Starter ($199/mo) | Pro ($499/mo) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly cost | $199 | $499 |
| Monthly recovered revenue | $2,750 | $2,750 |
| **Net monthly gain** | **+$2,551** | **+$2,251** |
| ROI multiple | **13.8x** | **5.5x** |
| Months to payback setup ($399) | <1 month | <1 month |

## The Pilot Anchor (Use This on the First Call)

> "We'll forward only your after-hours calls for 30 days. Those calls are currently going to voicemail. After 30 days, we'll show you exactly how many leads we captured and what they were worth. If the math doesn't work, you walk."

This works because:

- After-hours calls are **already lost revenue**, no risk
- The prospect doesn't have to fire their receptionist or change workflow
- 30 days produces enough data to show ROI
- The owner sees real numbers, not estimates
