## Bottom line

- **Pricing ($199 / $499 / $999 + setup): VALIDATED.** Sits squarely in the competitive US band.
- **Comp offer (50% yr-1 + 5% lifetime): generous but rational** for pure commission-only independents.
- **Channel bet: CHALLENGED.** Leading with commission-only reps (the CommissionCrowd plan) should NOT be the primary motion. Lead with a **white-label/agency reseller channel**; run commission-only reps as a cheap parallel experiment.
- **Precondition under both channels:** with one reference customer and an unproven repeatable motion, the highest-leverage first step is **founder-led selling** to prove the 30-day after-hours pilot converts. Good reps AND good agencies both want evidence it sells before they invest.

## 1. Channel decision (the key finding)

### Why commission-only shouldn't be the primary motion
- Heavy, credible skepticism of pure commission-only 1099 reps in B2B SaaS. Jason Lemkin (SaaStr): _"never seen 1099 reps work in B2B and SaaS"_, too many vendors, too much competition, too little incentive when reps can't close instantly. Experienced reps resist pure commission. _(medium confidence; opinion/norm-grade)_
- **Nuance:** that critique targets W-2 employee hires and short cycles. Genuine independent 1099 channel partners self-select for upside and accept commission-only as normal. So this **tempers, not forbids** the CommissionCrowd plan, it argues against it being the _only_ bet.

### Why white-label/agency reseller is structurally better
- A reseller channel already exists for this exact product category (My AI Front Desk, Synthflow, Autocalls, Kickcall, Viirtue). Resellers buy at wholesale (~$55/receptionist, publicly disclosed) and set their own retail ($49–$999/mo), keeping the spread. The partner owns pricing and margin → incentives align without policing a comp plan. _(high confidence, primary vendor pages)_
- **Strategic catch:** those platforms are also your competition, agencies can already get a generic AI receptionist to resell for ~$55. Your wedge: **purpose-built for home services, deployed, real reference customer.** You're entering a channel with incumbents, not an empty field.

## 2. Pricing validation

| Competitor | Model | Pricing |
|---|---|---|
| **Smith.ai** (AI Receptionist) | per-call subscription | $95 / $270 / $800/mo self-service; $500 / $1,000 / $2,000/mo done-for-you (annual) |
| **Abby Connect** (AI tiers) | included-minutes | $99 (50 min) / $165 (100) / $299 (200) / $690 (500)/mo; **$95 one-time setup** |
| **Our plan** | included-minutes + overage | $199 / $499 / $999/mo + $299–499 setup |

- **$199 entry** = slightly premium over the ~$95–99 market floor. Defensible for a home-services-specific product. _(high confidence)_
- **$999 Premium** = defensible for high-volume accounts.
- ⚠️ **Setup fee flag:** competitors charge $0–$95–$299. Ours ($299–499) is at/above the top of the range. It filters tire-kickers (intended), but **consider waiving it during the 30-day pilot** to cut friction for reps.
- ⚠️ **Outcome model ($35–40/booked appointment) is UNBENCHMARKED.** Zero verified competitors price per-booking, all use per-call, per-minute, or included-minutes. Double-edged: a possible differentiator (de-risks the buyer) OR a sign the market isn't educated on it and reps will struggle to explain it.
  - **Recommendation (agrees with `PRICING.txt`):** keep per-booking as the _pilot/trial_ mechanism ("60 days pay-per-booking, then move to a plan"); make **subscription the steady state**, what buyers and reps understand without education.

## 3. Commission structure validation

- Salaried-SaaS norm ≈ **10–15% of ACV** (Bridge Group 2024 median: 11.5% across 172 firms). Our 50% yr-1 + 5% lifetime is far above that.
- **But** that norm assumes a base salary. **Pure commission-only independents legitimately command 15–40%+ per sale plus residuals** to offset zero base. _(medium confidence)_
- **Verdict: generous but defensible** as a founder-stage recruitment hook.
- Myth busted in verification (refuted 1-2): "pre-PMF startups must use a base-heavy 70/30 split." No obligation to add a base you can't afford.

## 5. Recommended sequence

1. **Phase 0, Founder-led (now).** Close **3–5 more clients yourself** with the 30-day after-hours pilot wedge. Precondition for everything else; turns "we think it sells" into a script reps can run. (Missed-call ROI stats strongly support the wedge.)
2. **Phase 1, White-label/agency program (primary scalable channel).** Once the motion is proven, recruit 1–2 agency/MSP partners with own-brand reselling. Low fixed cost, aligned incentives.
3. **Phase 2, Commission-only test (cheap, parallel).** Run a **$500–750 experiment on CommissionOnly.com** (pay-per-app, no lock-in) or the existing **CommissionCrowd Premium** post. Treat it as a hypothesis test, "can an independent rep sell the pilot?", not the lifeline.

## 6. What the research could NOT answer (gaps to close with real pilots)

- **Demand-side willingness-to-pay**, all pricing data is supply-side (what competitors charge), not what pest/HVAC/plumbing owners will pay. Pilots are how you learn this.
- **Whether per-booking ($35–40) actually converts**, no competitor benchmark exists.
- **What margin agencies require to resell**, the "30% minimum" claim was refuted; real partner economics for this vertical are unresolved.
- **Realized close rates / ROI on the rep platforms**, costs known, outcomes unknown.
