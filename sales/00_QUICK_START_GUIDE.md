# Sales Quick-Start Guide
### AI Phone Receptionist for Home-Service Businesses
*Read time: ~10 min. This is the overview, deeper assets are linked at the bottom.*

---

## 1. The product in one line

> A 24/7 AI receptionist that answers the phone, qualifies the caller, captures the booking, logs it, and texts a confirmation, so the business stops losing jobs to missed and after-hours calls.

It is a **live phone receptionist**, not a chatbot. Built on real voice tech (Twilio, Deepgram, Claude, Cartesia). Currently live with a pest-control reference customer (Clear Slope).

---

## 2. Who to sell to (this matters most)

**Don't target by industry, target by the buying pattern.** It shows up in dozens of verticals.

**✅ GREEN LIGHT, chase any business where ALL of these are true:**
1. Inbound **phone** is the main way they book jobs (not online forms only)
2. Each booking is worth **real money** (~$150+ a job)
3. Calls get **missed routinely**, after-hours, on the job, overflow
4. A missed caller just **calls a competitor** instead of waiting
5. The **owner decides**, no procurement committee

Best size: **growing owner-operators, ~3–12 trucks/techs**, ~$500K–$3M revenue, where the owner (or one overwhelmed person) still answers the phone and hasn't hired a dedicated receptionist yet.

Fits: pest control (warm start, we have the case study), HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, garage doors, towing, locksmiths, auto repair, restoration, property management, and more.

**🚫 RED FLAGS, log it, but bring to the founder before promising anything:**
- **Healthcare / dental / medical** → HIPAA. We can't serve these yet. Do **not** promise.
- **Legal** → high liability/expectations. Founder-handled only.
- Anyone needing **on-call payment or binding price quotes** → not built yet.
- **Very low ticket (<$75/job)** → the ROI doesn't clear our pricing cleanly.
- **20+ truck operators** with a full reception desk + ServiceTitan → they'll demand integrations we haven't shipped. Revisit later.

**Too-small (1–2 trucks):** low volume, can't pay, churns fast. Skip.

---

## 3. Why they buy (the pain, in one breath)

Lead with money they're **already losing**, not features:

- Home-service businesses miss **20–38% of inbound calls** (worse in peak season)
- **78% of callers won't call back**, they call the next company
- A missed call is worth ~**$1,200** in lost revenue on average
- **20–40% of calls** come in **after hours**, going straight to voicemail

**ROI in numbers** (use the [ROI Sheet](02_ROI_SHEET.md) to fill in live):
- Small operator recovers **~$2,750/mo** in otherwise-lost bookings
- Mid-size operator recovers **~$5,670/mo**

The frame that closes: *"This costs a fraction of a human receptionist (~$3–4K/mo) and never sleeps, never misses a call, never quits."*

---

## 4. The sales motion: lead with the risk-free pilot

The fastest close is the **30-day after-hours pilot.** It removes all risk because those calls are *already* going to voicemail.

> "Forward only your **after-hours** calls to the AI for 30 days, the ones already going to voicemail. We'll send a weekly recap showing calls handled, leads captured, and estimated recovered revenue. If the math doesn't work, you walk."

Then **convert to a plan** once they see real captured volume. Land risk-free → expand to subscription.

---

## 5. The pitch (a script you can adapt)

**Opener:**
> "Quick question, when a customer calls after hours or when you're on a job, what happens to that call right now?"

*(They'll say voicemail / nothing / they lose it.)*

**Pain:**
> "Right, and about 8 out of 10 of those people won't leave a message or call back. They just dial the next company. For a business your size that's usually a few thousand dollars a month walking out the door."

**Value + pilot:**
> "We put a 24/7 AI receptionist on those calls. It answers live, qualifies the caller, books the job, logs it, and texts you. Let's prove it with zero risk: forward just your after-hours calls for 30 days. We show you exactly what we captured. If it's not worth it, you stop. Fair?"

Keep it conversational. The goal of call #1 is to **book the pilot**, not to close a contract.

---

## 6. What to quote

> ⚠️ **Confirm current pricing AND your commission split with the founder before quoting.**

Two ways to start the client:

- **Performance pilot (best de-risk wedge):** ~$35–40 per booked appointment. Pay only for results, no monthly minimum.
- **Subscription plans:** Starter / Pro / Premium (monthly + one-time setup). See [Pricing One-Pager](01_PRICING_ONEPAGER.md).

Recommended play: **land on the pilot, convert to a subscription** once volume is proven (recurring is better for them to budget and for us). Always anchor price against the **~$3–4K/mo cost of a human receptionist**, not against "cents per minute."

If a prospect is a great fit but needs a vertical we haven't built, that's a **founder conversation**, we may build it for the right signed deal.

## 7. Top 5 objections (full set in [Objection Handling](04_OBJECTION_HANDLING.md))

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Will it sound robotic / embarrass me?" | "Let me play you a real call. It's a natural voice, and the pilot lets you hear every call before you commit." |
| "It's too expensive." | "Compared to one missed job at ~$1,200, or a $3–4K/mo receptionist? The pilot only charges when it books you real work." |
| "I already have voicemail / an answering service." | "Voicemail loses 78% of callers, and answering services just take a message. This books the job, 24/7." |
| "What if it makes a mistake?" | "It qualifies and logs everything, flags emergencies, and texts you, you review it all. Start with after-hours only: those calls are already lost." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Totally fair, that's exactly why the pilot is risk-free. It only touches calls already going to voicemail. Let's just measure it for 30 days." |

---

## 8. Hard rules (don't break these)

- **Never** pitch it as "replace your receptionist" on day one, that creates fear. Pitch it as **capturing calls they're already missing.**
- **Never** promise a red-flag vertical (HIPAA/healthcare, legal, payments), escalate to founder.
- **Don't** over-promise a brand-new industry as day-one perfect; the first client in a new vertical is a tuning partner.
- **Don't** quote pricing or terms you haven't confirmed with the founder.
- Lead with **the prospect's lost revenue**, not our tech stack.

---

## 9. The close & handoff

1. Get a **yes to the 30-day pilot** (low bar, it's free of risk).
2. Collect: business name, call volume, who answers now, hours, the number to forward.
3. Hand off to the **founder for setup** (number provisioning, prompt config, onboarding).
4. Founder runs the pilot + weekly recap. You stay in the loop for the **convert-to-subscription** conversation at day 30.
