# AI Phone Receptionist for Home Services
## Pricing & Plans

24/7 call answering, lead capture, and booking, built for pest control, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and similar local service businesses.

---

### How It Works

- Calls forward to a dedicated business number (or replace your main line)
- AI receptionist answers in your business's voice, qualifies the caller, captures booking details
- Bookings logged to Google Sheets in real time; SMS confirmation sent to caller
- Owner gets daily and weekly recap of calls handled and leads captured
