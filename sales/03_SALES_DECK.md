# Sales Deck Spec, AI Phone Receptionist
## 10-slide deck for home-service prospects (pest control, HVAC, plumbing, roofing)

Format: drop the content into Google Slides. Each slide below has the title, on-slide message, visual direction, and speaker notes. Keep slides visual, bullets are summaries, not scripts.

**Total length on a call:** 15–20 minutes including the demo. Cuts to 8–10 minutes for a tight pitch.

## Slide 2, The Problem

**Title:** Your Phone Is Leaking Money
**Bullet 1:** Pest control businesses miss **20–38% of inbound calls** *(PCN 2026)*
**Bullet 2:** **78% of callers don't call back** after one unanswered ring *(Nextiva 2026)*
**Bullet 3:** **20–40% of service calls arrive outside 9–5** *(industry estimate)*
**Bullet 4:** Each missed call costs ~$1,200 in lost revenue, not counting lifetime value *(Aira 2026)*

**Visual:** Big number, "62%" or "27%", depending on how aggressive you want to be. Show one stat at a time if presenting live. Faded background of a ringing phone.

**Speaker notes:** Most owners know they miss calls. They don't know how many. The industry data is brutal, 1 in 4 to 1 in 3 inbound calls go unanswered, and most of those callers don't try you again. They call the next guy on Google.

---

## Slide 3, What That Costs You

**Title:** A 5-Truck Operator Loses ~$47K/Year to Missed Calls
**Math (on-slide):**
- 25 calls/week × 4 weeks = 100 calls/month
- 30% missed = 30 missed calls
- $275 avg ticket × 45% conversion = **$124 per captured call**
- **Monthly revenue lost: ~$3,960**
- **Annual revenue lost: ~$47,500**

**Visual:** A vertical bar showing "Captured Revenue" vs "Leaked Revenue", make the leak look big and red.

**Speaker notes:** I won't pretend to know your exact numbers, but if you tell me your weekly call volume and average ticket, I'll do this math live. Most operators are surprised by the number. A 15-truck operation? PCN's study puts it at $281,000 a year.

---

## Slide 4, The Solution

**Title:** A 24/7 AI Receptionist That Sounds Human
**Bullet 1:** Answers every call live, no voicemail, no ring-out
**Bullet 2:** Qualifies the caller, captures booking details, logs to Google Sheets
**Bullet 3:** Sends SMS confirmation to the caller after the call
**Bullet 4:** Tags emergencies, respects your business hours, knows your service area

**Visual:** Product mark + 4 icons (phone, qualify, log, SMS). Or a screenshot of the Google Sheets log filling in real-time.

**Speaker notes:** This isn't a chatbot. It's a real phone receptionist that picks up, has a conversation, captures what matters, and logs it. The caller can't tell the difference in most cases, and if they want a human, it can transfer.

---

## Slide 5, How It Works (30-Second Diagram)

**Title:** From Ring to Booking in 90 Seconds

**Diagram flow (left to right):**
Customer calls → AI answers → AI qualifies (name, address, service, urgency) → Booking logged to Google Sheets → SMS confirmation to caller → Daily/weekly recap to owner

**Visual:** Linear flow diagram with simple icons. Keep arrows clean. Add a tiny "<5 second response" badge.

**Speaker notes:** That's the entire flow. The caller never knows they hit an AI, they get a normal conversation. You get a clean record of every lead, in a tool you already use.

## Design Notes for Google Slides

- Stick to 2 fonts max (one display, one body)
- Use a single accent color (suggest green for "money saved" / "captured")
- Don't fill slides with text, most should have one visual focal point
- Include slide numbers in footer for reference during Q&A
- Export to PDF for email follow-up after the call
