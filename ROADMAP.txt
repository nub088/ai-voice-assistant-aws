AI VOICE RECEPTIONIST, PRODUCT ROADMAP
Last updated: 2026-05-02

GUIDING PRINCIPLE

  Build to demand, not imagination. Every item below is sized
  by revenue impact and ranked by how directly it removes a
  sales objection or unlocks a tier upgrade. Speculative items
  are flagged "WAIT FOR PULL."

  Same logic that applies to the routing add-on (see PRICING.txt)
  applies to everything in NEXT and LATER: don't build until a
  paying customer or a signed LOI is on the table.

================================================================
NEXT, Features that close deals (3–6 months, demand-driven)
================================================================

Build any of these when a prospect names it as a deal-blocker. Don't
pre-build. Get an LOI first if possible.

  ─── Owner-visibility features (highest deal-close impact) ──────

  • CLIENT DASHBOARD
    Owner sees: live call list, transcripts, recordings, booking
    log, monthly minute usage, trend charts.
    Why it sells: owners don't trust black-box AI. A dashboard is
    the #1 thing they'll ask about on a demo.
    Effort: 2–3 weeks. Pitch as "included in Pro tier and above."
    Pricing impact: enables Pro/Premium upsell vs. Starter.

  • CALL RECORDING + SEARCHABLE TRANSCRIPTS
    Twilio records, Deepgram transcripts already exist, bundle into
    the dashboard with search/filter.
    Why it sells: training material, dispute resolution, QA.
    Effort: 1 week (mostly UI on existing data).

  • DAILY EMAIL DIGEST TO OWNER
    "Yesterday: 12 calls, 4 bookings, 1 emergency, 2 missed."
    Why it sells: owners want a glance, not a dashboard, on day-to-day.
    Effort: 3–5 days.

  ─── Revenue-expansion features ─────────────────────────────────

  • SMART SCHEDULING + ROUTE OPTIMIZATION (see PRICING.txt)
    +$249/mo add-on. Wait for client pull.
    Effort: 1–2 weeks (likely 3–4 with edge cases).

  • OUTBOUND CALLING, appointment confirmations + reminders
    Day-before reminder calls reduce no-shows by 20–40% in home
    services. Owners will pay real money for this.
    Why it sells: directly recovers revenue lost to no-shows.
    Effort: 2 weeks (Twilio outbound + scheduling cron + opt-in
    handling for TCPA compliance, important).
    Pricing: +$99–$199/mo add-on, OR per-call ($0.50–$1.00/outbound).

  • OUTBOUND, lapsed customer reactivation
    Quarterly call/text to past customers: "It's been 90 days since
    your last service, want us to schedule a follow-up?"
    Why it sells: highest-ROI marketing channel for service businesses.
    Effort: 1–2 weeks beyond the outbound foundation above.
    Pricing: bundle into Premium tier or +$199/mo.

  ─── Integration features (each unlocks a vertical) ─────────────

  • CRM / FSM INTEGRATIONS
    HouseCall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, FieldEdge.
    Why it sells: these are the tools every serious operator already
    uses. Integration = "we fit into your stack."
    Build order: whichever the first 3 prospects use. Don't pre-build.
    Effort: 1–2 weeks per integration. Each is a distinct codepath.

  • SPANISH-LANGUAGE SUPPORT
    Pest, lawn, construction industries have heavy Spanish-speaking
    customer base. Cartesia + Haiku both handle it; mostly a prompt
    + voice-config swap.
    Effort: 3–5 days. Charge nothing extra; it's a competitive must.

  • SMS FALLBACK
    Caller hangs up? Send SMS: "Sorry we couldn't connect, here's a
    booking link." Recovers ~10–20% of dropped calls.
    Effort: 1 week.


================================================================
LATER, Speculative; revisit at 10+ paying clients
================================================================

WAIT FOR PULL on all of these. None should ship before there's a
clear customer ask AND it doesn't cannibalize a higher-tier feature.

  • Custom voice cloning (owner's own voice for Premium tier)
    Cartesia supports voice clones. Premium-tier-only sticker.

  • On-call quote / estimate generation
    LLM uses pricing rules to quote on the call ("a one-bedroom
    treatment runs $180–$240"). Risk: liability if quote is wrong.
    Needs strong guardrails and an opt-in disclaimer.

  • Payment collection on call
    Stripe-style "$50 deposit to hold the slot." Big trust uplift,
    big compliance surface (PCI, refund handling).

  • Lead quality scoring
    Tag each call: hot / warm / cold based on intent signals.
    Feed into CRM. Useful for owners with sales follow-up teams.

  • Reputation / review collection automation
    Post-service: "Hi, this is the AI from ABC Pest, would you
    leave us a Google review?" Drives review velocity.

  • Multi-vertical expansion (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing)
    Each vertical = new prompt template + taxonomy + pricing book.
    Don't expand until pest is at 5–10 clients and the playbook
    is repeatable.

  • White-label / agency reseller program
    Marketing agencies serving home services would sell this for
    you. 30–40% revenue share. Only viable once self-serve
    onboarding is solid (don't sell what you can't deliver).

  • Compliance certifications (SOC 2, HIPAA)
    SOC 2: needed for mid-market deals (>$2K MRR). 6-month, $20K+
    process. Pull only when a deal requires it.
    HIPAA: unlocks medical/dental verticals (much higher ACVs)
    but adds material engineering and audit overhead.


================================================================
INFRA / OPERATIONAL, Behind-the-scenes, no client-facing pitch
================================================================

These don't appear on the pricing page but make everything else
possible. Schedule them in proportion to client count.

  • Prompt A/B testing framework, once you have 5+ clients, you'll
    want to compare prompt variants on real call outcomes (booking
    rate, call length, drop rate).

  • Call quality analytics, automated detection of: long silences,
    repeated questions, off-topic drift, customer frustration.
    Feeds prompt improvement.

  • Automated regression testing, replay past calls against prompt
    changes (replay harness already exists in repo). Expand it.

  • Per-client cost dashboards, internal view, not for clients.
    Catch margin erosion early.

  • Staging environment health monitoring, already partially in
    place (STAGING.md). Round out before scaling clients.


================================================================
WHAT'S NOT ON THIS ROADMAP (and why)
================================================================

  ✗ Building our own LLM / fine-tuning Haiku
    Premature. Haiku 4.5 + good prompting beats fine-tuning until
    you have >100K calls of data and a clear performance gap.

  ✗ A native mobile app for owners
    Owners check email and dashboards, not new apps. Web dashboard
    is enough through year 1.

  ✗ Real-time call transfer to a live human
    Twilio supports it, but adds operational complexity (who's on
    call? what happens after-hours?). Wait for explicit demand.

  ✗ Building our own STT/TTS
    Cartesia and Deepgram are commodity-priced and improving faster
    than we can build. Don't reinvent.
