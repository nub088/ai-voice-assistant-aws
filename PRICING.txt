UNIT COST (what it costs us to run a call)
----------------------------------------------------------------
~5–6 cents per minute, all-in. Breakdown:

  Twilio Voice (US local DID)        $0.0085/min
  Twilio Media Streams               $0.0040/min
  Deepgram STT (Nova streaming)      $0.0043/min
  Cartesia TTS (Sonic streaming)     $0.020–0.030/min
  Claude Haiku 4.5 (cached prompt)   $0.005–0.010/min
  Twilio SMS (per message, optional) $0.0079
  Google Sheets append               ~$0
  ----------------------------------------------------
  Variable subtotal                  $0.045–0.055/min

  Fly hosting (shared-cpu-1x, 1GB)   ~$10–20/mo fixed
  → adds ~$0.001–0.015/min depending on volume

Profitable on a single client at any tier from day one.
No "wait until we scale" caveat.

Hidden gotchas:
  - Toll-free numbers cost ~3x local ($0.022/min). Charge as add-on.
  - Deepgram, Cartesia, Fly DO NOT support hard billing caps.
    Mitigate with Twilio call-duration limits + per-client minute
    ceilings in app code.
  - Long calls amplify Cartesia + Anthropic non-linearly
    (TTS scales with character count).

PRICING TIERS (what we charge clients)
================================================================

Standard 3-tier subscription with included minutes + overage.

  ┌────────────┬──────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
  │ Tier       │ Price/mo │ Included min │ Overage rate │
  ├────────────┼──────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
  │ Starter    │ $199     │ 500          │ $0.40/min    │
  │ Pro        │ $499     │ 1,500        │ $0.30/min    │
  │ Premium    │ $999     │ 4,000        │ $0.25/min    │
  └────────────┴──────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘

  Plus one-time SETUP FEE: $299–$499
    Covers prompt customization, Twilio number provisioning,
    Sheets integration, and onboarding call.
    Filters tire-kickers, pays for the manual onboarding labor.

GROSS MARGIN at full utilization:
  Starter:  ($199 - $25 cost) / $199  = ~87%
  Pro:      ($499 - $75 cost) / $499  = ~85%
  Premium:  ($999 - $200 cost) / $999 = ~80%

  Effective margin after support, churn replacement, payment fees,
  onboarding labor: ~65–75%. Still strong SaaS territory.

WHY SUBSCRIPTION (not per-minute):
  - SMB owners (pest, HVAC, plumbing, roofing) buy in monthly
    line items, not metered usage.
  - Sales rep can anchor a plan name, not cents-per-minute math.
  - Predictable MRR is what makes the business salable later.
  - Matches Smith.ai, Ruby, Synthflow, no buyer education needed.
  - Overage rate protects margin during surge months.

OPTIONS WE'RE NOT USING (and why):
  - Pure per-minute ($0.50–$1.00/min): kills forecasting, makes
    sales commission messy, customers churn the first slow month.

OUTCOME-BASED PRICING (first-client model, decided 2026-05-21):
----------------------------------------------------------------
  $35–40 CAD flat per booked appointment.

  Why flat rate (not percentage):
    - Ticket value varies ($120–$300+). Percentage creates disputes.
    - Flat rate = no ticket tracking, no arguments, clean invoice.
    - ~17.5–20% of $200 CAD average, target range without the math.

  Terms:
    - Invoiced weekly from Google Sheets booking log.
    - Cancellations: client still owes the fee. We booked the job.
    - No monthly minimum. Pay only for results.

  Why this works for seasonal businesses (pest control, HVAC, etc.):
    - Busy season = more calls unanswered = highest AI value.
    - Client has no incentive to renegotiate during surge, the AI
      is capturing revenue they'd otherwise lose entirely.
    - API cost per booked call is ~$0.52–$2.60 CAD (5–10 min call).
      Margin is $32–39 CAD per booking at any realistic conversion rate.

  Conversion estimate (inbound, high-intent callers): 30–50%.
  Attribution: fully logged in Sheets, call, booking flag, contact.
  No human confirmation needed; AI captures or it doesn't.

  Path to subscription: use outcome pricing as a trial period
  ("60 days, pay per booking, then we discuss a plan"). De-risks
  the sale; converts once the client sees their monthly volume.

ADD-ON (FUTURE): SMART SCHEDULING + ROUTE OPTIMIZATION
================================================================

THE FEATURE
  When a customer calls to book, the assistant checks staff
  availability AND drive-time between the new appointment and
  existing scheduled jobs, then offers slots that minimize
  windshield time. For pest/HVAC/plumbing where techs do
  4–8 visits/day, this is a real ROI lever.

COST IMPACT (when we eventually build it)

  Google Maps Platform per booking call:
    Geocoding (address → lat/lng)        $0.005
    Distance Matrix (3–5 elements)       $0.015–0.025
    Routes API (advanced, traffic)       $0.010 (optional)
                                         ─────────────
    Per booking total                    $0.020–0.040

  Per-minute call cost added:            $0.001–0.003/min
  LLM tool overhead (Haiku):             +$0.001–0.002/booking

  Free tier: ~10,000 free Google Maps calls/mo per API.
  Effectively $0 cost for first ~5,000–10,000 bookings/mo.
  Won't pay anything for routing until ~50 active clients.

  Calendar integration (Google Calendar API): free.

PRICING FOR THE ADD-ON

  Add-on rate:  +$199–$299/mo on top of any base tier
    OR
  New top tier: "Premium+" at $1,499/mo includes 5,000 min + routing

  Why this much: optimized routing realistically saves a 5-tech crew
  $200–500/day in productive billable time. $249/mo is an obvious yes.

  Margin on add-on revenue: ~95% (variable cost is near zero).

CONSTRAINTS TO NAME IN THE SALES PITCH
  - Customer must keep tech schedules in a connected calendar
    (Google Calendar, Calendly, supported FSM tool).
    Manual whiteboards = no integration possible.
  - Address geocoding has ~5–10% miss rate for rural / new-build
    addresses. Need a manual override path.
  - Position as "smart booking," NOT "fleet management." Won't
    beat ServiceTitan or HouseCall Pro on full route optimization.

WHY WE'RE NOT BUILDING THE ADD-ON YET
================================================================

DECISION: Wait until a client asks for it. Don't build speculatively.

REASON 1, Launch blockers come first.
  Per memory (multi_client_and_ship_readiness_plan.md), there are
  3 blockers + 6 important items still open from the launch audit.
  Twilio is also still on a trial account (must upgrade before
  client #2). Building a new feature on top of an un-shipped
  foundation is how products die.

REASON 2, Speculative builds get the wrong abstractions.
  The estimate says 1–2 weeks. Reality once you hit edge cases:
  3–4 weeks. Lunch breaks, no-show buffers, multi-day jobs, techs
  starting from home vs. office, after-hours emergency overrides,
  geocoding misses in rural ZIPs. None of those are visible until
  a real operator uses it. Build it without a customer and you'll
  rip half of it out for the first one who actually does.

REASON 3, Roadmap promises are valid sales currency.
  "Smart routing is on our Q3 roadmap, included in Premium+ when
  it ships" is a normal SaaS sales line. If a prospect won't sign
  without it, you've found the customer worth building it for,   and now you have a paid spec.

THE MIDDLE PATH (if the rep needs ammo):
  Build a 2–3 day demo mock, a Loom or slide showing the
  conversation flow ("I see Tuesday at 2pm puts you next to
  our Wednesday job in your area, would that work?"). Sells
  the vision without committing the engineering.

TRIGGERS TO BUILD IT FOR REAL
  1. A prospect explicitly says "I'd buy this if it had routing"
     - get a signed LOI before you start coding.
  2. ClearSlope (existing reference customer) asks for it. Real
     spec, deepens the reference, retention upside.
  3. Two or three clients independently raise it during onboarding
     - that's market signal, not noise.

Until one of those fires, every engineering hour is better spent on:
  - Closing the 3 launch-readiness blockers
  - Multi-client tenancy / onboarding automation
  - The sales sheet and demo script for the rep
  - Inbound support for the rep's first prospects
